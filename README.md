## Overview

One project, three connected experiences, all served by a single FastAPI server:

| Where | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **`/`: Deccan House website** | A premium, cinematic brand site with a 3D coffee cup, a 3D café walkthrough, an editorial menu, story, gallery, events and a real reservation system. | Anyone discovering the café |
| **`/table?t=N`: ChAimate** | The AI companion at every table. You scan the QR code and just talk: it chats in 11 Indian languages, looks up live facts and takes orders by voice. | Guests at the café |
| **`/staff`: staff app** | Kitchen and bar tickets, the floor, bills, the menu, analytics and an AI store manager. | Kitchen, bar, waiters, manager |

**ChAimate** (said *chai-mate*) answers out loud through the table speaker or the guest's own earphones. It remembers returning guests (if they choose to sign in) and sends each ordered dish to the right station: food to the kitchen, drinks to the bar. AI chat time is metered: every visit gets 10 free minutes, then ₹2 a minute appears on the bill.

## Design: Deccan House

The website and ChAimate share one art direction: understated luxury with a quiet Hyderabadi identity.

- **Palette:** espresso `#11100E`, charcoal `#1B1916`, ivory `#F5F1E9`, cream `#F4EFE7`, beige `#D8CBB9`, and a single bronze accent `#B78A5A` / `#D3A878`, used sparingly.
- **Type:** *Cormorant Garamond* for large editorial display type (uppercase with an italic accent), and *Manrope* for the interface, with letter-spaced labels.
- **Form:** near-square corners, hairline rules instead of boxes, generous whitespace and a subtle film grain.
- **Hyderabad, subtly:**
  - The logo is a single Qutb Shahi pointed arch.
  - "Rooted here" draws an arcade of arches and minarets in bronze line art as you scroll.
  - The 3D café has pointed arched windows, and the copy speaks of Deccan hospitality, not tourist clichés.
- **3D:**
  - **Website hero:** a porcelain cup on Nero Marquina marble, with steam, a rippling surface and latte art. The cup, the light and the camera follow your cursor.
  - **Website walkthrough:** a cut-away model of the café with hotspots (Coffee Bar, Private Dining, Outdoor Seating, Bakery, Work Lounge) that fly the camera to each space.
  - **ChAimate:** a hand-thrown clay kulhad is ChAimate's face. It breathes while listening, its spices whirl while thinking, and its steam and chai ripple with the voice while speaking.
- **Motion:**
  - Lenis smooth scrolling and GSAP ScrollTrigger reveals (word by word, image masks, parallax).
  - A pinned horizontal reel, magnetic buttons and a custom cursor that reads VIEW / EXPLORE / BOOK / TALK.
  - Curtain page transitions and a full-screen mobile menu.
- **Every device:**
  - Phones and low-memory devices get lighter 3D.
  - People who turn off motion get stills or photographs.
  - 3D loads only when it's near the screen.
  - Photos are served as AVIF or WebP at four sizes, lazy-loaded, with blurred placeholders.

## Features

**For customers**
- 🎙️ **Hands-free voice conversation:**
  - Voice detection spots when you start and stop talking; you can talk over the companion at any time.
  - While it thinks you hear a short "hmm" or "achha", and live captions show what's being said.
  - It works on the table speaker, AirPods or Bluetooth earphones.
- 🗣️ **One companion, many personalities:** ChAimate speaks 11 Indian languages, with 7 personalities (Chai Buddy, Filmy Friend, Cricket Fanatic, Wise Elder…), 6 moods, 12 topics and 8 Sarvam voices. Customers can give it a nickname.
- 🧠 **Memory for returning customers** (phone and PIN):
  - Facts it learns ("supports RCB", "less sugar"), a summary of each visit, their usual orders.
  - Opinions it has shared, so it stays the same friend.
  - Customers can delete any memory, or everything.
- 🛒 **Reliable ordering:**
  - The server, not the model, decides which dish was meant: "discuits" becomes Osmania Biscuits, and a vague "chai" gets asked about.
  - Every cart change shows a "🛒 Added 1 × Irani Chai · Undo" chip.
  - The order is read back before it's sent to the kitchen.
- 🍛 **An 89-dish menu in 8 sections:**
  - **Trending Now**, the Gen Z favourites: momos, Korean corn dogs, ramen, loaded fries, waffles…
  - **Hyderabadi Specials:** mandi, pathar ka gosht, nihari, Apollo fish, Chicken Majestic, shawarma…
  - **The rest:** Hot Drinks (with Sulaimani and pink chai), Cold Drinks (with boba, cold brew and matcha), Breakfast, Snacks, Meals and Desserts.
  - **Photos:** 79 dishes have a real, credited photo; the rest show an illustrated tile.
  - **For existing cafés:** new dishes are added to an existing café's menu once, on start-up. Dishes it already has are skipped, and a dish the manager deletes is never brought back (`MENU_CATALOG_VERSION` in `app/core/db.py`).
- 📖 **Swiggy-style menu:** real photos, veg/non-veg marks, ★ Bestseller tags (top 3 sold in 30 days), search, a veg-only switch, an ADD button that becomes a − 1 + stepper, and a floating "View cart" bar.
- 🌐 **Live internet facts:** releases, scores, news and weather, with strict "only say what the results show" rules.
- 💬 **Proactive, like a friend:**
  - It mentions when your order is ready.
  - After a long silence it checks in, at most twice in a row, and never while the AI is rate-limited.

**For staff**
- 🍳 **Station routing:** each order is split between the kitchen and the bar. It becomes *Ready* only when every station is done, and waiters get an alert.
- 👥 **Role-based access:** kitchen, bar, waiter and manager each see only what they need.
- 📊 **Analytics:** revenue including AI chat, busy-hour heatmap, top and falling items, lapsed regulars, prep times, and automatic plain-language findings.
- 🤖 **AI store manager:** briefings and chat grounded in the café's own data. Its proposals (prices within ±50%, menu changes, promotions) wait in an approval queue.
- ⏱️ **AI usage metering:** live conversations, minutes, tokens and LLM cost. Settled AI minutes go on the table's bill.

## Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  subgraph Browser
    W["Deccan House website<br/>/ (Next.js static export)"]
    C["ChAimate<br/>/table?t=N"]
    S["Staff app<br/>/staff"]
  end
  subgraph Server["FastAPI (app/)"]
    API["REST routers<br/>app/api/routes"]
    RT["Voice conversation engine<br/>app/realtime/conversation.py"]
    HUB["Live hub<br/>app/realtime/hub.py"]
    AG["Agents<br/>companion · store manager"]
    GW["LLM gateway<br/>LiteLLM router"]
  end
  C -- "WebSocket: audio, text, cart taps" --> RT
  W -- "reservations · events · newsletter" --> API
  W -- "Talk to ChAimate" --> C
  C -- HTTPS --> API
  S -- HTTPS --> API
  HUB -- "WebSocket: live orders" --> S
  RT --> AG --> GW
  GW --> GROQ["Groq<br/>gpt-oss-120b → 20b"]
  RT --> SARVAM["Sarvam AI<br/>speech-to-text · text-to-speech"]
  AG --> WEB["Web search<br/>ddgs"]
  API & RT --> DB[("SQLite or<br/>Supabase Postgres")]
  RT --> REDIS[("Redis<br/>metering")]
  DB -. "Supabase Realtime" .-> HUB
```

**The website** is a Next.js site exported to static HTML/JS/CSS (`frontend/out`). FastAPI serves it at `/`, so everything runs from one process on one port. Its forms call the same API, and a new reservation shows up live on the staff screens.

**One voice turn:** the mic audio is checked for speech in the browser, and a 16 kHz WAV is sent over the WebSocket. Sarvam turns it into text. The companion LLM streams its reply, using tools for cart, orders, search and memory. The reply is split into sentences, each sentence goes to Sarvam text-to-speech, and the audio plays back while the next sentence is still being generated.

## Getting started

### Prerequisites
- Python **3.9+**
- **Node.js 20+** and npm, to build the Deccan House website (not needed for ChAimate or the staff app alone)
- **Redis** (for metering): `brew install redis && brew services start redis`, or `docker run -d -p 6379:6379 redis:7`
- A **Groq** API key: <https://console.groq.com/keys>
- Optional: a **Sarvam AI** key for Indian voices (<https://dashboard.sarvam.ai>). Without it, the browser's built-in voice is used.

### Quick start
```bash
git clone <your-repo-url> cafe-ai && cd cafe-ai
make install          # creates .venv, installs requirements-dev.txt, copies .env.example → .env
# edit .env: set GROQ_API_KEY (and SARVAM_API_KEY, STAFF_PIN)
make web              # builds the website into frontend/out (npm install + next build)
make dev              # http://localhost:8000
```

| Open | URL |
|---|---|
| Deccan House website | <http://localhost:8000/> |
| ChAimate (table 1) | <http://localhost:8000/table?t=1> |
| Staff app | <http://localhost:8000/staff> |
| Interactive API docs | <http://localhost:8000/docs> |

If the website hasn't been built, `/` simply redirects to ChAimate at `/table?t=1`.

### Working on the website
```bash
make dev              # terminal 1: the API on :8000
make web-dev          # terminal 2: Next.js dev server on http://localhost:3000 (hot reload)
```
`make web-dev` sets `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_BASE=http://localhost:8000` so forms reach the API; CORS already allows `localhost:3000`. Inside `frontend/`, `npm run lint` and `npm run typecheck` check the code. Run `make web` again to publish changes to `:8000`.

Content such as the menu, prices, address, events and testimonials lives in [`frontend/lib/content.ts`](frontend/lib/content.ts). To add photos, drop a `.jpg` into `frontend/assets/photos/`, credit it in `CREDITS.json`, and rebuild.

### Try it without any API keys
```bash

DB_PATH=data/demo.db make dev
```

### Phones: use HTTPS so the microphone works
Browsers only allow the microphone on secure pages, so `http://192.168.x.x` can never capture voice on a phone.
```bash
make https            # → https://<your-wifi-ip>:8443/table?t=1
```
`scripts/https.sh` creates a self-signed certificate for your Wi-Fi address (in `certs/`, which is git-ignored) and serves over HTTPS on your local network only. Open the link on the phone and accept the certificate warning once. Then set `PUBLIC_BASE_URL=https://<ip>:8443` so the printed table QR codes point there.

## Configuration

Every setting lives in `.env`. See [`.env.example`](.env.example) for the full, commented list.

| Variable | Default | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `GROQ_API_KEY` | — | Groq key (required for the real companion) |
| `LLM_MODEL` / `LLM_FALLBACK_MODEL` | `groq/openai/gpt-oss-120b` / `…-20b` | main and instant-fallback models (LiteLLM `provider/model` names) |
| `LLM_BACKUP_MODEL` | empty | optional third model on another provider, e.g. `cerebras/gpt-oss-120b` plus `CEREBRAS_API_KEY` |
| `LLM_REASONING_EFFORT` | `low` | `low` gives the fastest spoken replies |
| `SARVAM_API_KEY` | empty | Indian speech-to-text and text-to-speech; empty means the browser's voice |
| `REDIS_URL` | `redis://localhost:6379/0` | metering store |
| `AI_FREE_MINUTES` / `AI_PRICE_PER_MINUTE` | `10` / `2` | free chat minutes per visit, then the price per minute |
| `DATABASE_URL` | empty | Supabase/Postgres session-pooler URI; empty means SQLite at `DB_PATH` (default `data/cafe.db`) |
| `SUPABASE_URL` / `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY` | empty | optional Realtime feed (the service key is **server-side only**) |
| `CAFE_NAME` / `CAFE_CITY` / `CURRENCY` | Deccan House / Hyderabad / ₹ | branding and the AI's context |
| `COMPANION_NAME` | `ChAimate` | the AI companion's name as shown in the app; it says it plainly ("Chaimate") so voices pronounce it *chai-mate* |
| `STAFF_PIN` | `1234` | the manager's PIN; **change it** |
| `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` | `http://localhost:8000` | the base address printed into table QR codes |
| `LOG_LEVEL` / `LOG_FORMAT` / `LOG_DIR` | `INFO` / `text` / `logs` | logging (`json` for log platforms) |
| `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_BASE` | empty (same origin) | **website build only**, the API address when the site is hosted apart from FastAPI (set in `frontend/.env.local`) |

<details>
<summary>Using Supabase instead of SQLite</summary>

1. Create a project at supabase.com. Choose the **Mumbai (ap-south-1)** region for the lowest latency in India.
2. **Connect → Session pooler** (port 5432): copy the URI into `DATABASE_URL`, with your database password in it. The session pooler works on IPv4; the free tier's direct connection is IPv6-only.
3. Optional: **Project Settings → API** → put the Project URL in `SUPABASE_URL` and the secret key in `SUPABASE_SERVICE_KEY`, to get live staff-screen updates when the database is edited anywhere.
4. Start the app. On first start it creates:
   - its tables in a private `cafe` schema,
   - Row Level Security on every table, with access revoked for Supabase's public `anon` and `authenticated` roles,
   - Realtime on orders, order items, staff requests, the menu and tables.
5. Optional: run `python -m app.seed` against it. It refuses to run if the database already has orders.
</details>

## Using the apps

**Website:** browse the menu, story, gallery and events, then book a table under **Reservations**. The flow has three steps (date and guests, a live time slot, your details) and ends on "Your table is waiting" with a `DH-XXXX` reference and an "Add to calendar" (.ics) file. Slots run every half hour from 7:30, with the last seating at 21:30 (22:30 on weekends). Each slot holds up to 40 covers, and slots less than 45 minutes away can't be booked. Every booking reaches the staff app live. **Talk to ChAimate** in the nav or footer opens the table app.

**Customer:** scan the QR code, then tap **Talk to ChAimate** and just talk. Other options:
- **📖 Menu:** order by tapping.
- **💬 Chat:** see the transcript, or type instead of talking.
- **⋯:** change the companion, call a waiter, ask for the bill, or see what it remembers.

Logging in ("I've been here before") is optional. It's only needed for the companion to remember you.

**Staff:** go to `/staff` and log in with a role:

| Role | Default login | Sees | Can do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manager | `manager` / `STAFF_PIN` (1234) | everything, plus sales, analytics, the AI manager, staff and customers | edit the menu, manage staff, approve AI proposals, cancel orders |
| Kitchen | `kitchen` / 1111 | food tickets only | Start → Ready, mark its own items out of stock |

## How it works

<details open>
<summary><b>Voice pipeline and turn-taking</b></summary>

- **Voice detection:** runs in the browser and adapts to room noise. A turn ends after about 1 s of silence, or about 1.3 s after a short "umm", with a 30 s limit per recording.
- **Barge-in:** talking over the companion stops it. While it is speaking, the detection is stricter so its own voice from the speaker doesn't interrupt it.
- **Streaming replies:** the reply is voiced sentence by sentence, starting at the first clause break, so the companion starts talking in about a second.
- **Self-healing:**
  - A blocked mic is never saved as "Type only"; one tap on the mic tries again.
  - Audio is unlocked inside the first tap, which is what iPhones require.
  - A "Thinking…" state that hangs for more than 25 s resets itself.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Ordering you can trust</b></summary>

The model names the dish, and `match_menu_item()` finds it on the menu:
- **Typos and variants:** it copes with typos, plurals and spoken variants.
- **Vague requests:** it returns the choices, so the companion asks which one.
- **Unknown dishes:** a dish that isn't on the menu is reported honestly.
- **Direct taps:** menu taps (`cart_set`) edit the cart without an AI round trip. When the menu is closed, the companion acknowledges what was added in a few words.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Sounding human</b></summary>

- **Opening lines:** a different opening "angle" each visit.
- **Fillers and backchannels:** varied fillers while it searches, and cached "hmm/achha" clips in the chosen voice.
- **Honesty:** a rule against invented experiences ("I was listening to…"); opinions are allowed.
- **Good company, not a waiter:** most of the chat is about the person. ChAimate follows up on specifics, asks open questions that invite a story, calls back to earlier details, and gives sincere, specific compliments. It never steers toward food: menu, cart and promotions come up only when the guest mentions food, asks for a suggestion or says they're hungry, with at most one casual suggestion a visit otherwise. The quiet-moment check-ins are never about ordering.
- **Carrying on the conversation:**
  - **Signed-in guests:** they get their recent messages back, plus person-focused summaries of past chats ("they were nervous about Friday's interview — ask how it went"). ChAimate greets them by picking up a thread from their life.
  - **Guests without an account:** their chat is kept in server memory per table for up to 3 hours, so a reload, a dropped connection or End → Talk carries on where it left off. It's cleared when the table's bill is settled.
- **Order-ready lines:** it reacts to the actual dish ("your biryani's coming!") rather than reading out an order number.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>LLM gateway and rate limits</b></summary>

- **Routing:** LiteLLM routes to the primary model, then the fallback, then the optional backup. A failing model is cooled down so the next customer skips it.
- **Rate limits:** if every model is rate-limited, the gateway waits about 4 s and makes one direct retry on the lighter model, because per-minute budgets refill fast.
- **Token saving:** web-search results from earlier turns are cut to a short excerpt, and the system prompt keeps a stable, cacheable prefix.
- **Free-tier limits:** Groq's free tier allows 8K tokens per minute and 200K tokens per day per model, which is fine for testing. Use the pay-as-you-go Developer tier, or add a backup provider, for real customers.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Metering and billing</b></summary>

- **What counts:** a chat minute is a clock minute in which the table actually talked with the AI; silences are free.
- **Where it's tracked:** Redis tracks minutes, tokens, cost and speech usage per visit.
- **Settling:** settling a table writes the usage to the database and adds any paid minutes to the bill.
- **Privacy in the live monitor:** by default it shows metadata only. Set `MONITOR_CHAT_TEXT=true` to include short snippets of what was said.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>AI store manager guardrails</b></summary>

- **Read-only analysis:** it looks up data with read-only tools; every change is a proposal in an approval queue, and every decision is logged.
- **Prices:** changes are capped at ±50%, and it won't raise prices on items whose sales are falling.
- **Promotions:** they can't promise prices or discounts that the bill wouldn't match. Approved promotions are mentioned by the table companion.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Logging and error handling</b></summary>

- **Log files:** `logs/cafe.log` and `logs/errors.log`, rotated at 10 MB with 5 kept, or JSON lines for log platforms.
- **Request IDs:** every request carries an id (the `X-Request-ID` header), and every voice session gets a tag such as `[t2-5473ad]`.
- **Redaction:** API keys, tokens and passwords are masked in every log line.
- **Error responses:** errors are returned as JSON `{detail, code, request_id}`, and tracebacks never reach the browser.
- **Browser errors:** errors from both apps are sent to `/api/client-log`, rate-limited.
- **Health check:** `/api/health` reports ok, degraded or down, for uptime monitors.
</details>

## Testing & quality

```bash
make test        # 44 tests: unit, API and realtime — needs a local Redis
make lint        # ruff
```
- **Isolation:** tests run against a temporary SQLite database, Redis database 15 and a temporary log folder.
- **No external calls:** the LLM and Sarvam are replaced by scripted fakes (`tests/conftest.py`), so tests never touch Supabase or spend API quota.
- **Coverage:**
  - Menu-name matching, gateway rate-limit retry, prompt and context trimming.
  - Error bodies, request ids, redaction, transactions.
  - Accounts and memory across visits; station routing and role permissions.
  - Ordering by name and Undo; the proactive check-in limit and order-ready announcement.
  - LLM outages, malformed or oversized audio, and handler crashes.
- **Website:** `cd frontend && npm run lint && npm run typecheck`. `npm run build` also fails on type errors.
- **Screenshots:** `make screenshots` re-creates the README images (Playwright): ChAimate and staff shots from a seeded demo café, plus the website shots when `frontend/out` exists.

## Deployment

- **Single process:** run `uvicorn app.main:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000` behind a TLS reverse proxy (Caddy or nginx), or on a platform such as Render, Railway or Fly.io. HTTPS is required for the microphone.
- **Website:** run `make web` before deploying, since `frontend/out` is git-ignored. To host it separately (Vercel, Netlify, a CDN), deploy `frontend/out` and build with `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_BASE=https://your-api`.
- **Workers:** use one worker process. Live sessions and the WebSocket hub are held in memory, so scaling out needs a shared pub/sub (for example Redis) first.
- **Services:** use Supabase (or any Postgres) and a managed Redis, and set `LOG_FORMAT=json` for log platforms.
- **Before opening:**
  - Rotate every key that has ever been shared.
  - Change `STAFF_PIN` and the default staff logins.
  - Set `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` and print the table QR codes (`/api/tables/{id}/qr.svg`, also under *QR codes* in the staff app).

## Security & privacy

- **Secrets:** only in `.env` (git-ignored). The Supabase service key never reaches the browser.
- **PINs:** hashed with PBKDF2, with a 5-minute lock-out after 5 wrong PINs. Login cookies are HTTP-only and SameSite=Lax.
- **Supabase:** tables live in a private schema with Row Level Security, and public roles are revoked.
- **Memory control:** customers can see and delete what the companion remembers, or erase all their data.
- **Staff monitor:** shows metadata only unless `MONITOR_CHAT_TEXT=true`.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| The phone never hears you | Open the app over **HTTPS** (`make https`). Plain `http://` blocks the mic, and the error log shows `microphone unavailable: TypeError`. |
| "The AI is busy (rate limit)" | Groq's free-tier limits were reached. Upgrade to the Developer tier or set `LLM_BACKUP_MODEL`. The app already retries once and falls back to the lighter model. |
| No voice from the companion | Tap the 🔊 prompt (the browser blocked audio), check the phone's silent switch, or set `SARVAM_API_KEY`. |
| Metering shows "unavailable" | Start Redis (`brew services start redis`). |
| `/` goes straight to ChAimate | The website isn't built. Run `make web`, then restart. |
| The website shows old content | Rebuild with `make web`, then hard-refresh. |
| The map is blank | The map tiles come from OpenFreeMap. Check your internet connection, and rebuild if `public/vendor/maplibre` is missing. |
| An old design after an update | Restart the server; assets are versioned on every start. Hard-refresh once (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+R). |
